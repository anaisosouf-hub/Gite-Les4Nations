Surroundings

Nestled in the heart of Normandy's Pays d'Auge region, our gites offer the perfect base for discovering the area's rich heritage, stunning landscapes, and culinary delights.

🏰 Château de Crèvecœur

10-15 minutes away

A beautifully preserved medieval castle and museum showcasing Norman heritage and oil production history. Perfect for history enthusiasts and families.

📍 Get Directions

⚓ Honfleur

45 minutes away

A picturesque harbor town with a charming old port, art galleries, and excellent seafood restaurants. Don't miss the Vieux Bassin and Saint Catherine's Church.

📍 Get Directions

🍎 Calvados & Cider Route

15-20 minutes away

The Pays d'Auge is famous for Calvados (apple brandy) and cider production. Tour local distilleries, taste regional products, and discover traditional Norman craftsmanship.

🍎 Visit Website

Also nearby: Deauville, Cabourg, and the beautiful Côte Fleurie beaches are just 45 minutes away!
